Daily Post

Official Documentation & Information Page

About This Tool

Daily Post is a desktop tool for TikTok creators who produce original short videos on their own computer. It sends a creator's own videos to their own TikTok account as drafts, so the creator can review, edit and publish them in the TikTok app.

How It Works

  1. Sign in with your TikTok account through TikTok OAuth and grant the requested permissions.
  2. Select an original video and caption you created on your computer.
  3. Daily Post uploads the video to your TikTok inbox as a draft, using TikTok's Content Posting API.
  4. Review, edit and publish the draft in the TikTok app. Daily Post never publishes content directly.

Who Can Use Daily Post

Any TikTok creator can use Daily Post with their own TikTok account. The tool is not tied to a specific account: each user signs in with their own account, and only their own content is uploaded to their own account.

Data and Privacy

Daily Post runs locally on your device and does not operate a server that receives your videos or account data. Access tokens are stored only on your own device. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for details.
