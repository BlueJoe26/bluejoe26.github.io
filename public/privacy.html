Privacy Policy

Daily Post does not collect, store, or share users' personal data on any server operated by us.

The tool runs locally on the user's own device. When a user signs in with TikTok OAuth, their access token is stored only on their own device. Videos and captions are sent only to TikTok, through TikTok's official API, when the user instructs the tool to upload them as drafts to the user's own TikTok account. No user data is transmitted to us or to any third party other than TikTok.

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us through this website.
